Ini sa Sur-Sur

The logo "Ini sa SurSur" represents the voice, identity, and shared stories that define Surigao del Sur. The phrase "Ini sa SurSur" translates to "This is Surigao del Sur" or "This is who we are in SurSur."

The speech bubble symbolizes conversation, storytelling, and information-sharing. The quotation marks emphasizes real voices and lived experiences.The bold and large "SUR" visually anchors the logo to Surigao del Sur, asserting provincial identity and pride. The three dots suggest continuity and openess, that the stories, practices, and information about Surigao del sur are ongoing and ever-evolving.

The slogan "It's a SUR Thing" plays on the word "sure", expressing confidence, reliability, and trust. It reinforces the core identity of Ini sa SurSur by highlighting what is distinctive, natural, and proudly local about Surigao del Sur.

KAT-ON sa SurSur

What does KAT-ON stand for?

KAT-ON stands for Knowledge Advancement and Transformation for Operational Needs

What is this about?

A Knowledge Sharing Program of DILG Surigao del Sur designed to transform routine processes into opportunities for Knowledge and skills enhancement, empowering personnel to work smarter and deliver results with confidence.
